#### [FX Exposure Lab](https://github.com/divyanshatpar05/fx-exposure-lab)
How much would a strong dollar cost Nike, and would hedging help? Models currency risk for 12 multinationals on **live ECB exchange rates**.

- Monte Carlo simulation of 11 correlated currencies → Value-at-Risk
- Forward vs. option hedging with a risk-vs-cost frontier
- Replays 8 historical crises; rate shocks flow through a DCF valuation

#### [Growth Lens](https://github.com/divyanshatpar05/growth-lens)
Revenue grew 27%, but where did it come from? Splits growth into **8 drivers**: new, churn, cross-sell, volume, mix, price and more.

- Shapley-value attribution, so the answer doesn't depend on calculation order
- Tenure-aligned cohorts, automatic anomaly detection, scenario planner
- Upload your own CSV; every bridge reconciles to the cent

**[▶ Live demo](https://growth-lens.onrender.com)** · `Python` `Pandas` `FastAPI` `React` `Plotly`
